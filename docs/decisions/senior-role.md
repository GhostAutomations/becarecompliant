# Senior role

> The "Senior" role Phil wants on every BCC company (2026-09-29) — a BUILT-IN default role (not a custom add-on), names only, ticks in Role access. Read before touching roles, Role access or the Senior role.

- [stated] 2026-09-29: a role called just "Senior" (some call it senior carer, but the name is Senior), on all companies.
- [stated] CORRECTION same day: NOT an add-on/custom company role (the 0314 kind). "I want senior to be a default role" — a built-in role listed in Role access like Supervisor/Viewer. The custom-role version (0336) was wrong and is to be undone.
- [stated] "All they should be able to see is a list of people's names basically."
- [stated] Popup decisions: names from their own branch(es) only; in Role access the Senior tile has tick boxes "People / Service users" to choose which name lists they see; they keep their own carer portal (holidays, training, forms to sign, RTW questions); NOT a paid user (free like carer logins).
- [stated] Addition same day: in the Senior tile, under People and under Service users, a box per Check (the company's own checks). When People / Service users is ticked, its boxes are active and ticked. A ticked Check = the Senior sees its status and due date next to each name AND can open and complete its form. A Check the company adds later starts ticked.
- [stated] A new Senior joins like any new employee: added as a Person with an email (Team Member invite), then changed to Senior in Settings, Users. No Senior choice on Add a person.
- [stated] Senior tile layout: People on the left, Service users on the right.
- [stated] Senior tile layout (final): People across the top with its Checks in 3 columns, dashed line, Service users below in the left column; tile the same height as Viewer; Senior sits between On Call and Viewer.
- [stated] A Senior's People / Service Users page: each name is its own gold button; clicking the name opens the form (or forms) they are to do. No wall of rows of every Check. Rejected: stacked rows per person with status pills and Complete buttons.
- [stated] "Forms I have sent in" in a carer's own area is only for their own personal forms (holiday and the like), never Checks they completed on service users or other people; they must not be able to reopen those completed forms.
- [stated] NAMES CHANGED same day ("the names need to match to save confusion"): login roles take the job-title names. "Team Member" login is now shown as "Care Assistant"; "Senior" is now "Senior Care Assistant". The Settings section is "Carer logins" and the record box "Carer login". Job titles unchanged.
