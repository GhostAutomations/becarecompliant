# Signatures on letters (Job 2 Part A)

Phil, 2026-10-12, standing:

- Why there were none: letters were never built to carry one. No office login had a stored
  signature, and the letter PDFs drew only "Yours sincerely", the name and the job title.
- [stated] Which letters: the two PDF letters, the absence meeting invitation (and its rearranged
  copy) and the absence meeting outcome letter. Complaint responses as PDF letters are parked as
  THE LIST 54.
- [stated] Who has "My signature": Supervisor, Recruiter, Manager, Registered Manager, Registered
  Individual and Company Admin. It lives in the profile panel under the initials circle, top right.
  A login can keep an uploaded signature and a drawn one.
- [stated] Choices on a letter: my saved uploaded signature, my saved drawn signature, sign now
  (drawn for this letter only), or No signature. No typed signature: the printed name sits under it.
- [stated] The sign off follows the signer: whoever signs gets their own name and job title under
  their signature. With No signature, the person holding the meeting stays in the sign off as before.
- A saved signature belongs only to its owner: nobody else can see, use or download it, enforced in
  the database and storage (private, short lived signed URLs). The Founder in support mode cannot
  use anyone's. Every use is audit logged. A letter already sent never changes.
