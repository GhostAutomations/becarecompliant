# Outcomes

> BCC Outcomes sub-department (Service Users): personal outcomes -> PQS %; agreed design + build progress

Outcomes = a sub-department under Service Users (nav: Service Users > Outcomes, /service-users/outcomes was a placeholder "coming later"). Purpose: capture each service user's PERSONAL OUTCOMES (Welsh well-being / CIW outcomes framework — "what matters to me") and roll up the % achieving or progressing for the PQS (Cardiff/LA Provider Quality System return, see [cardiff-pqs](cardiff-pqs.md)).

DESIGN AGREED (Phil, 2026-07-21, all recommended): (1) per-SU LIST of outcome statements, each with a STATUS = Achieved / Progressing / Working towards / No longer relevant, + last_reviewed date + review note; (2) recurring OUTCOMES REVIEW with RAG due dates (like care plan reviews), completing it stored as Evidence; (3) Outcomes overview page = register of SUs + outcomes status + headline PQS % (achieving or progressing), exportable PDF/CSV.

PQS rule: an outcome is IN SCOPE unless "no longer relevant"; "achieving or progressing" = Achieved or Progressing. % = achieving-or-progressing / in-scope.

## Not achieved, and the past close date (Phil, 2026-10-14, Thistle ticket 8; 0466)

- [stated] An outcome closes **Achieved** or **Not achieved**. Not achieved needs a reason (the database refuses one without); it shows on the outcome history with a grey "Not achieved" pill, in the audit log, and in the subject access export's outcome notes (popup "Everywhere Achieved shows"). It is not part of the care plan review form.
- [stated] **The counting rule, in one place** (`isOutcomeAchievingOrProgressing` and `isOutcomeClosed` in `lib/service-users/outcome-consts.ts`): in scope = every outcome not removed; met = Achieved or Progressing. Not achieved is closed, in scope, NOT met. Removed outcomes (Remove, archived_at, which is what "no longer relevant" became in 0103) stay out of every count. Used by the register, the PQS percentage, the Cardiff return, readiness, the dashboard tiles, Reg 73 and 80 and the record page.
- [stated] **Close date**: with the past box ticked (shown only while the company's past entries window is open), "Date it was closed" is required, for Achieved and Not achieved; without it the close date is today, as before. Not in the future, not before the outcome began (or was added), not before its latest dated update, and for a discharged person not after the discharge (`closeDateError`). Stored as the close update's `dated_on` and the outcome's `achieved_at` at midday, so it never shifts a day.
- [stated] Popup "Smallest change": status `not_achieved`, update kind `not_achieved` (its note is the reason), no new columns; `achieved_at` keeps meaning "when it closed" for both. The SAR export's Achieved column shows a date only for an Achieved outcome.
- [stated] Popup "A Mark not achieved button beside Mark achieved": Mark achieved is unchanged apart from the past box; closed Not achieved outcomes collect in their own "Not achieved outcomes (n)" section. Reopen works the same for both.
