# Office matrix and Lead the Leader (LTL) in place of supervision

Phil, 2026-10-14 (job spec plus popups). Applies to all companies, current and future, Demo
included.

## What it is

- A tick box "Show on Office matrix" on a login (Settings > Users). Every tick and untick is audit
  logged.
- [stated] No new People tab: "in branches, there is an option to view [the office branch]. So
  change the company name to Office in branches. And anyone that's got the ticked box will move from
  the branch that they're in to the office branch."
- The People matrix with the Office branch chosen shows LTL 1, LTL 2, LTL 3 (Due and Done) in place
  of Supervision 1 to 3. Annual Appraisal stays exactly as it is.
- LTL runs on the same engine and rules as supervisions (appraisal mode): LTL 1 due one supervision
  interval after probation end or the last appraisal, LTL 2 after LTL 1, LTL 3 after LTL 2, the
  appraisal after LTL 3. Same pill rules. The company's supervision interval, never a hard coded 80.
- Carry on from where they are: when ticked, their supervision history is their place in the LTL
  cycle; when unticked, LTLs done while ticked are their place in the supervision cycle.
- While ticked: completed with the existing Lead the Leader form; not chased for supervisions; LTL
  and appraisal count everywhere supervisions do (dashboard, rollups, readiness, reports, exports,
  morning email, reminders, chasers).
- Not ticked: Lead the Leader stays exactly as now (ad hoc, no due date, not on any matrix).

## Popup answers (2026-10-14)

- Unticking puts them back in the branch they came from (remembered when ticked).
- Who sees Office people: Founder, Admin, Registered Manager, and anyone given access to the Office
  branch. Supervisors do not see it. Enforced in the database like every branch.
- The All branches view leaves Office people out; they show only when Office is chosen.
- The office branch is renamed "Office" in every company, and new companies' office branch is
  created as "Office".
- The tick is allowed only on a login linked to a People record (greyed out otherwise, refused in
  the database).
- Only LTLs done while ticked count towards the cycle; earlier ad hoc LTLs stay ad hoc extras.
- PQS Supervision percentage (and the Regulation 73 and 80 figures that use it): Office people stay
  in it, judged on their LTLs.
- Four supervisions mode: the Office matrix follows the company, LTL 1 to 4 and no appraisal.
- Only Admin and the Founder can tick or untick (Settings > Users stays Admin only; the job spec's
  Registered Manager was asked and answered "Admin and Founder only").
- Ticking is refused for a job title Lead the Leader does not apply to (the check's job titles).
- The Planner Whiteboard lists Office people's LTLs to book in place of supervisions.
- After ticking (Phil, 2026-10-14: "when I click in it says supervision"): the Lead the Leader form
  says "Lead the Leader meeting" where it said "supervision" (8 places, every company and the
  template, 0469; the sentence about a carer's supervision stays). Used forms got a new version, so
  completed Lead the Leader Evidence keeps the wording it was filled in with.
- A ticked person whose probation end was set without the Probation Review form had no stored LTL 1
  date, so nothing chased it: stored by hand for the Thistle supervisor (Phil's go); everyone else
  like it is THE LIST 59.
