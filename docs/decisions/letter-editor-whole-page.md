# The whole letter on the editor page, and job titles (Phil, 2026-10-12)

Standing decisions, from Phil's review of Book meeting in Chrome ("the one on the left must match
exactly, so people aren't trying to edit something that doesn't need editing").

- [stated] The editor page shows the whole letter as the PDF prints it: letterhead, date, Private and
  confidential, address, Dear and the RE line above the words; Yours sincerely, the signature (or
  room to sign), the name and the job title below. Only the words in the middle can be typed in.
  The fixed parts fade a little under the pointer. Every letter editor: Book meeting, the outcome
  letter (box and meeting form) and the two letter templates in Settings, Letters (with example
  details, and the meeting details and absences shown where the letter adds them).
- [stated] The meeting details (Date, Time, Length, Where, Held by) show as the PDF draws them, bold
  labels lined up with no colons, and are LOCKED: they follow the fields above the letter, cannot be
  typed over or removed, and the server puts the booking's details back into any edited letter.
- [stated] The job title under a name on a letter is set by an Admin (or the Founder in support
  mode) for each login on Settings, Users, "Job title on letters" (0447). Locked in the database like
  the name, audit logged. Blank means the name alone. A job title on the person's People record is
  used when the login has none. Never the system role (Part C). The top right menu shows it too.
- [stated] Under the editor, the signature box; beside it, under the PDF, what Approve or Save does
  and the buttons, centred in a box the same height (outcome letter and Book meeting).
