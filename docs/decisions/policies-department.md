# Policies department

> Phil's decisions (2026-10-06, Phase 13 current phase) for Policies as its own BCC department with an AI policy writer/improver, guidance library and policy writer roles. Read before touching Policies.

- [stated] Policies moves out of Settings into its own department called "Policies" (Briefings still sends policies out for signing); chose to build it in the current phase, not Additions.
- [stated] Companies can still upload policies, and also create a policy or have their policy improved with a new AI tool expert in ACAS, CQC, NHS, CIW, Social Care Wales and other relevant bodies.
- [stated] AI expertise comes from a curated guidance library (sources with link and checked date, AI cites them), re-checked every 28 days; changes need Phil's approval before companies see "guidance changed, review this policy".
- [stated] First version includes: write a new policy, improve my policy, owner + review date + RAG register on the dashboard, required policies checklist by regulator.
- [stated] Admins write and approve; Managers view and send. AI drafts/checks spend AI credits and are metered like other AI tools.
- [stated] In Role access, Company Admins can tick Managers, Registered Managers and Registered Individuals to write and approve policies like an Admin (Policies only, not wider admin access).
- [stated] HR policies get their own checklist section on Policies (separate from "Policies your regulator expects", own count), covering all four sets: core (grievance, sickness, holiday, family leave, flexible working, bullying/harassment), performance (capability, probation, code of conduct), time off and hours (dependants/carer's leave, bereavement, working time), wellbeing and conduct (drugs/alcohol, social media, stress/wellbeing/menopause, expenses/mileage).
- [stated] Policy library founder page needs an "approve all" option rather than approving each source change one by one; a source with no readable text must not be approvable.
- [stated] AI policy credits: writing a policy costs 3 AI credits, improving one costs 4.
- [stated] The AI sickness absence policy must follow the company's absence set up (stages or Bradford); probation policy must follow the probation set up. In Getting set up, absence and probation steps come before policies.
- [stated] Write with AI must ask company-specific details first (e.g. holiday entitlement days, what bereavement/compassionate leave the company offers).
- [stated] AI drafts: anything "to be completed" shows as its own field between the policy and "Save it as", not square brackets in the text. Write with AI asks who the policy owner is.
- [stated] Thistle's Responsible Individual wants policies ISO 9001 compliant: every policy written in BCC gets a cover page. Reference numbers automatic by area (editable); "Approved by" chosen, defaulting to the RI; Write page asks applies to, who must read and sign, retention, classification. Uploaded PDFs keep no cover.
- [stated] 2026-10-07: Thistle now also gets the full 57 policy register (previously Bevan only), replacing its 15 regulator + 18 HR checklist.
- [stated] Bevan uses the 57 policy register built from Thistle's policy list as its main Policies checklist, grouped by the register's sections, same on screen look; the 23 register policies with no standard yet were to be built as standard policies so Write with AI works for every row.
- [stated] When an AI answer can't be used (e.g. the review could not be read), the credits must be refunded.
- [stated] Policy cover must look like Thistle's own policy covers (example: their Recruitment Process and Procedure): page 1 logo large in the middle (no separate cover picture upload), company name and a coloured band with the title; colours from two colour pickers in Settings, Branding; page 2 the "Audit Checklist and Report" review table, then the ISO 9001 details; Reason for Review asked when approving.
- [stated] Policy reference numbers: every standard policy is CARE unless it is HR ("and care"); GEN only for non-standard policies.
- [stated] PDF tables (rule for all tables): a table that would split across pages moves whole to the next page; a row is only as tall as its text, never a fixed height.
- [stated] Policy cover footer must not carry "Uncontrolled when printed. The current version is held in Be Care Compliant." (removed at Phil's request).
- [stated] 2026-10-07 (after showing Thistle, testing in Bevan): a saved policy's PDF opens fine from Library, but there is no preview while creating one; wants a PDF preview during creation. Policies page tidy-up, Phase 13: register sections (Governance and quality, Person centred, Safeguarding...) collapsed, with "0 of 3 in place" beside the section heading instead of inside the card; Review register collapsed and its rows shorter in height (dropdowns moved up beside the title, not narrower); Library collapsed.
- [stated] 2026-10-07: remove "Add a policy" (the upload button under the Library heading) COMPLETELY, chosen knowing it was the only upload route.
- [stated] 2026-10-08 (reverses the 07 Oct removal): top of Policies has "Add current policy to library" (upload a PDF, or write or paste the wording, with how it is signed) in place of "Write a policy with AI" (Write with AI stays on each missing register line), with "Improve a policy with AI" next to it; the Add form also asks "Which standard policy is this?" so the register ticks straight away. Policies page sections are Settings > Users style dropdowns, register sections two per row; Review register left and Library right on one line.
- [stated] 2026-10-08: a fourth signing option "Draw and type" (both needed), wherever "How they sign this one" is offered.
- [stated] 2026-10-08: Improve a policy's "Which standard policy is it?" is optional, alphabetical, set from the chosen policy, with "Not one of the standard policies"; a non-standard policy is checked against the core care rules for the company's nation, and the review shows a "General check: core care rules" label at the top. Every "Which standard policy" list is alphabetical.
- [stated] 2026-10-08: policy cover Retention offers 3 to 10 years after it is replaced, 8 years the default.
